About Ocala Excavation Pros

Ocala Excavation Pros is a Marion County-focused excavation and land-clearing service. One number, one quote, one crew philosophy: land clearing, forestry mulching, grading, site prep, trenching, ponds and driveways for property owners from Ocala and Silver Springs Shores out to Dunnellon, Anthony, Citra and Summerfield.

How We Work

Everything on this site reflects how we think jobs should be run:

  • Free on-site estimates, written quotes. Marion County ground varies too much — Candler sand ridge vs. low ground near the Ocklawaha — for honest pricing over the phone. We walk the parcel, then put the number in writing.
  • Straight talk about your dirt. This county sits on the Ocala Limestone with deep, fast-draining sands on top. That geology decides how ponds hold, how pads compact and what clearing really costs — and we'd rather explain it before you spend than after.
  • Sunshine 811 locates on every dig. It's the law, it's free, and we call it in — no exceptions.
  • Fast callbacks. Call (352) 790-4301 any time. If we're on a machine, leave a message — you'll hear back the same business day. Prefer typing? The quote form on every page reaches us instantly.

What We Do

The full service list lives in the menu, but the short version: land clearing and forestry mulching, grading and drainage correction, building-pad site prep, trenching, pond work, driveways and demolition. If it involves moving Marion County dirt, start with us.
