Why Ocala Property Owners Call Us

  • Free on-site estimates with a firm written number. We walk the property with you, flag what stays and what goes, and put the price and scope in writing before a machine shows up.
  • We know Marion County dirt. Most of the county is deep, well-drained sand — Candler and Astatula fine sands — sitting over limestone karst. That sand digs easy but will not hold a compacted pad or a driveway without the right base work. We build for it because we work in it every week.
  • We plan for the rain. Ocala averages roughly 51 inches of rain a year, most of it in hard June-through-September afternoon storms. Every grade we cut answers one question first: where does that water go?
  • Licensed and insured, locates on every dig. Sunshine 811 utility locates are called in before trenching or excavation, every time.
  • We handle the paperwork questions. Marion County's tree protection rules, wetland jurisdiction (St. Johns River or Southwest Florida water management district depending on which side of the county you're on), and gopher tortoise surveys can stall a project. We tell you up front what your parcel needs.
