What Land Clearing Costs in Ocala, FL (2026)

Short answer: published cost guides put land clearing at about $1,200–$2,500 per acre for light clearing (brush, palmetto, small trees), $2,500–$4,500 per acre for moderate clearing, and $4,500–$8,000 per acre for heavy clearing with dense woods and large trees. These are national figures, not Ocala quotes. Stump grubbing, debris haul-off and access decide where a Marion County parcel lands. For the work itself, see land clearing in Ocala.

Typical Per-Acre Ranges From Published Cost Guides

  • Light clearing (brush, saw palmetto, small trees): $1,200–$2,500 / acre
  • Moderate clearing (thicker underbrush, medium trees such as mixed pine and oak): $2,500–$4,500 / acre
  • Heavy clearing (dense vegetation and large trees, such as an oak hammock): $4,500–$8,000 / acre
  • Forestry mulching alternative (mulch standing growth in place, no haul-off): usually the cheapest path on brushy acreage — see forestry mulching

About these numbers: the per-acre ranges above are typical ranges taken from published national cost guides (sources listed below). They are not quotes for your land and not one company's prices. Your actual price is set by the contractor after looking at your property in person, and it can fall outside these ranges.

What Actually Drives the Price

The three big variables are stump count, debris plan, and access.

  • Stumps and grubbing. Cutting a tree is fast; getting its root ball out of the ground is the labor. Dense oak parcels cost more almost entirely because of stumps.
  • Debris: haul, burn, or mulch. Haul-off is often the single biggest line item. Burning (where permitted) and on-site mulching both cut cost significantly.
  • Access and terrain. Tight gates, wet low ground, or a long carry to the road all add machine hours.
  • Season. June–September storms can soften ground and slow work; Marion County's deep Candler and Astatula sands otherwise dig easily.

How to Keep Your Clearing Bill Down

  • Clear selectively. Opening 2 usable acres of a 5-acre parcel costs far less than clearing the fence lines.
  • Keep debris on site. Mulch it or berm it instead of paying trucks.
  • Bundle the next step. If a pad, driveway or grading is coming, one mobilization beats two.
  • Get a written quote after a site visit. A contractor who has walked the land can price the stumps, debris and access instead of guessing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does it cost to clear 1 acre in Ocala?

Published national cost guides put moderate clearing at about $2,500–$4,500 per acre, light clearing at $1,200–$2,500 and heavy clearing at $4,500–$8,000. The actual price for your acre comes from the contractor's on-site quote.

Is forestry mulching cheaper than traditional land clearing?

Usually, yes — on brushy or lightly wooded acreage, mulching standing growth in place skips stump haul-off and debris trucking, which are the two biggest cost drivers. It leaves a mulch layer instead of bare dirt, so it fits pasture reclamation and view corridors better than building pads.

Does clearing cost more in wet season?

It can. June-through-September storms soften Marion County's sandy ground and can idle machines for a day, and schedules stretch. If your project is flexible, October through May quotes tend to hold tighter.

Do I need a permit to clear land in Marion County?

For most private-property clearing on uplands, no county clearing permit is required — but wetlands, gopher-tortoise habitat and work tied to a building permit all change the answer. Check with Marion County about your specific parcel before clearing.

What's the cheapest way to clear land?

Selective clearing with on-site debris handling: clear only the footprint you need, mulch or berm the debris instead of hauling it, and bundle grading into the same mobilization. Each of those choices cuts the amount of work you pay for.
