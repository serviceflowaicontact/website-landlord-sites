About Chattanooga Drainage Solutions

Chattanooga Drainage Solutions is a Hamilton County-focused residential drainage service. One number, one assessment, one plan philosophy: French drains, yard drainage systems, buried downspouts, sump pumps, regrading, dry creek beds and erosion control for homeowners from Chattanooga and East Ridge out to Hixson, Ooltewah, Signal Mountain, Soddy-Daisy, Red Bank and East Brainerd.

How We Work

Everything on this site reflects how we think drainage jobs should be run:

  • Free on-site assessments, written quotes. Chattanooga terrain varies too much — a flat East Brainerd lot drains nothing like a Signal Mountain slope — for honest pricing over the phone. We walk the property during or right after rain when we can, then put the number in writing.
  • Straight talk about your dirt. Most of Hamilton County sits on red clay over ridge-and-valley limestone, catching roughly 53 inches of rain a year. That combination decides where water goes, how deep a drain has to be and what a lasting fix really costs — and we'd rather explain it before you spend than after.
  • Tennessee 811 locates on every dig. It's the law, it's free, and we call it in — no exceptions.
  • Fast callbacks. Call (423) 401-9304 any time. If we're in a trench, leave a message — you'll hear back the same business day. Prefer typing? The quote form on every page reaches us instantly.

What We Do

The full service list lives in the menu, but the short version: French drains, yard drainage systems, buried downspout extensions, sump pumps, grading and slope correction, dry creek beds and swales and erosion control. If it involves moving water away from your home, start with us.
