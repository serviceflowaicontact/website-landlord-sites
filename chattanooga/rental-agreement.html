Website Lead-Generation Rental Agreement

chattanoogadrainagesolutions.com · between the site owner (“Owner”) and the renting company (“Tenant”)

Plain-English terms

1. What Tenant gets

Every customer inquiry the website chattanoogadrainagesolutions.com produces — phone calls to the site's tracking number and quote-form submissions — is routed exclusively to Tenant while this agreement is active. One tenant per market; Owner will not route these leads to anyone else.

2. What Owner keeps

The domain, website, content, tracking phone number, call recordings/transcripts, analytics, and search rankings are and remain Owner's property. Tenant's own Google Business Profile, customer relationships, and the jobs themselves are and remain Tenant's.

3. Rent & billing

Flat monthly rent at the rate agreed at signup, billed automatically to the card on file each month in advance. No setup fees.

4. Month-to-month, either way

Either party may end this agreement with written notice (email is fine), effective at the end of the paid month. On termination, lead routing stops; no refunds for the current month, nothing further owed.

5. No volume guarantee — and no games

Owner does not guarantee any specific number of leads; search traffic varies. In return, Owner plays it straight: leads are never shared, never resold, and never manufactured. Tenant may cancel the moment the math stops working.

6. Tenant's end of the deal

Tenant agrees to answer or promptly return lead calls (same business day), operate legally with any licenses/insurance the trade requires, and treat the site's callers professionally — the caller experience is what keeps the machine worth renting.

7. Recordings & privacy

Calls to the tracking number are recorded and transcribed with a notice to callers, for quality and lead-delivery purposes. Both parties will handle caller information lawfully and never sell it.
